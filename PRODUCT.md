# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two sides of a local, short-term gig marketplace (demo build, Austin, TX seed data):

- **Individuals** — people picking up short-term work (courier runs, event setup, cleaning, tech fixes, tutoring). Checking listings on a phone between commitments, deciding fast: "Does this pay enough, is it close, can I do it Saturday?"
- **Organizations** — local businesses and agencies posting one-off jobs and reviewing applicants.

Users are practical and time-pressed; the interface's job is to make pay, place, and time readable in seconds.

## Product Purpose

QuickGig connects people with short-term local jobs posted by real organizations. Post a gig in minutes, apply fast, verify and complete the work. Success = a visitor can go from landing → browsing → applying (or posting) without friction, and trust what they see.

## Brand Personality

Neighborly + efficient. Warm, candid, get-it-done energy. "You'll actually get paid and it won't suck." Confident but not salesy; the tone of a competent neighbor who shows up on time.

## Anti-references

- Overdramatic marketing: giant unverifiable claims, floating 3D blobs, scroll-novelas, parallax theater.
- Generic 2026 AI-SaaS look: purple gradient heroes, glassmorphism cards, glow shadows, gradient text.
- Cluttered job-board bureaucracy: dense beige tables, hostile filter walls, LinkedIn-corporate blue.

## Design Principles

1. **Pay, place, time are the home page.** The three facts users scan for get first-class visual weight in every listing surface.
2. **Show real artifacts, not claims.** Sample listings, real filters, honest empty states; no fabricated stats or testimonials.
3. **Clarity is the boldness.** Product register: decisive hierarchy, strong type contrast, one committed brand color — no theatrics.
4. **Neighborly, not corporate.** Warm display type, friendly copy, approachable shapes; still precise and dense enough to be efficient.
5. **Honest demo quality.** Where data doesn't exist (ratings, reviews), say so plainly instead of inventing UI.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA: body text ≥ 4.5:1, large/bold text ≥ 3:1 — verify every muted-on-tint combination.
- Full keyboard navigability with visible focus states; icon-only controls get `aria-label`.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected for every animation.
- Touch targets ≥ 40px; text rendered at user zoom without overflow.
